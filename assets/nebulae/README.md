# Nebulae and galaxy backgrounds

No galaxy or nebula GLB was imported in this asset batch. For real-time space backgrounds, use equirectangular HDR/LDR sky textures, cubemaps or procedural volumetric shaders; a galaxy is normally a large-scale background composition rather than a solid mesh. Keep any future downloaded nebula geometry under this directory and record its source and license in SPACE_ASSET_CREDITS.md.
